# Clearnym — Brief

This file is the source of truth for what Clearnym will and won't pitch, and how
the scan should be presented. It is prose on purpose: edit it like a note. The
detector loads it at triage and rendering time. When feedback changes what we
want, this file gets updated — not just one run.

## Audience

Our clients are regular people. Every pitch has to be simple and clearly about
**their own personal data** — what's exposed about them, who is selling it, and
how to get it removed. If a normal person can't read the headline and think
"that's about *my* data," it's not for us.

## We pitch

- A concrete consumer-data-exposure moment: a breach that hits individuals, a
  people-search / doxxing exposure, a removal or reappearance data point.
- "Here's what's public about you and how to take it down" service journalism.
- Anything where Clearnym can speak to the everyday mechanics of data removal
  with its own first-party numbers.

## We never pitch

These can never be pitch-ready. If the story is small, drop it to Watch and say
why. If it's a genuinely big story, keep it surfaced as an awareness item (never
hidden), but it is not a pitch.

- **Federal or state legislation, Congressional or regulatory process, policy
  debates.** Too abstract and policy-heavy for our audience, even when the
  subject is privacy. A subcommittee hearing is not a consumer story.
- **Industry infrastructure** — telecom, fibre, subsea cable, data-center, B2B
  or enterprise tooling. Off-category.
- **Partisan fights.** We stay a neutral consumer source; the moment we take a
  side we stop being a useful one.

## How to surface

- Lead with consumer-concrete pitch-ready items only.
- I don't need the "big stories / awareness-only" section expanded. If there's
  no consumer angle, a one-line count of what was held back is enough — don't
  hide it, just don't make me scroll it.

## Examples

- **BAD (too policy):** "Pallone slams Republicans' SECURE Data Act at House
  hearing." Federal legislation, abstract, no individual-data hook. _(2026-06-03)_
- **BAD (off-category):** "Google, Telstra partner on fibre and subsea networks."
  Infrastructure, no consumer-privacy angle. _(2026-06-03)_
- **GOOD:** "Report: the average person appears on 47 people-search sites."
  Concrete, about their own exposure, removable. _(2026-06-03)_
