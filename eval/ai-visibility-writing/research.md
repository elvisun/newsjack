# Research: writing-level AI visibility

Research date: 2026-07-21. This is an evidence map, not a best-practice list.
Platform behavior is time-sensitive; access dates matter. `Vendor` means the
publisher sells SEO, AI-visibility, or related services and its result is
treated as a claim until independently replicated.

## Lateral comparison of commercial practice

Commercial workflows converge on four operational moves: prompt-set audits,
source/citation monitoring, content changes, and repeated reporting. That
workflow is useful. Their prescriptive content rules do not converge reliably:

- Profound promotes FAQ/HowTo schema and universal word-count bands, while
  Google's official guidance says no special AI schema or machine-readable file
  is required.
- Organic/AIO overlap estimates vary from roughly 38% of citations in the top
  ten (Ahrefs, March 2026) to 94% of AIOs having at least one top-20 overlap
  (seoClarity, October 2025). The denominators differ, and both can be true; a
  single “overlap rate” is not portable.
- Vendors commonly combine mentions, sentiment, citation position, organic
  metrics, or traffic into a proprietary visibility score. That hides distinct
  outcomes and makes movement impossible to interpret.
- Advice to add FAQs, tables, exact word counts, or `llms.txt` is often based on
  correlations among already-successful pages. Authority, rank, topic, intent,
  and document type remain major confounders.

## Skeptical findings and causal limits

1. Live systems are unstable across repeated queries and minor paraphrases.
2. Source identity and publisher/domain effects can outweigh intrinsic prose.
3. Direct-query organic rank is important but incomplete under query fan-out.
4. A citation may not support the answer, and a cited page may contribute little
   to it; selection and accurate use are different outcomes.
5. Citation rarely implies a click, and none of the observational studies proves
   that rewriting an already-published page caused live visibility lift.
6. Most factor studies are vendor-authored, cross-sectional, and conducted on
   changing black-box products.

## Evidence map for the skill

| Factor | Evidence synthesis | Preliminary disposition | Safe product behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retrieval eligibility | Explicit official requirement on both Google and OpenAI surfaces. | Supported boundary, not a prose lever | Diagnose separately; do not promise prose can fix it. |
| Original/firsthand information | Google official guidance plus observational/preprint signals; authority confounding remains. | Promising | Ask for real proof or recommend an owned analysis page. |
| Direct scoped answer | Early controlled GEO work and descriptive absorption studies; modern live causal evidence is weak. | Promising | Move an already-supported answer forward; keep nuance. |
| Evidence and attribution | Early controlled GEO signal plus citation-quality literature; quantity can be gamed. | Promising | Tie claims to supplied primary evidence; never add decorative citations. |
| Entity clarity | Strong information-retrieval rationale but little direct live intervention evidence found. | Promising, low confidence and unvalidated | Clarify only genuine ambiguity; do not create repetitive entity stuffing. |
| Descriptive structure/chunks | Google guidance and observational absorption/vendor findings; intent and length confound. | Promising | Use one-purpose sections and descriptive headings, not universal FAQs. |
| Intent-fit tables/lists/steps | Descriptive evidence; format likely interacts with query/document type. | Promising, conditional | Use only for real comparisons/procedures. |
| Legitimate freshness | Large vendor correlation plus obvious query interaction; update detection and topic confound. | Promising, conditional | Surface real dates for current queries; never simulate freshness. |
| Neutral qualified prose | Early controlled work and editorial logic; limited modern live tests. | Promising | Replace unsupported promotion while preserving voice and qualification. |
| Readability/precision | People-first official guidance; no universal grade-level evidence. | Supported human guard | Improve clarity without erasing specialist precision. |
| Keyword repetition/checklist saturation | GEO paper and people-first/spam principles argue against it; human-quality harm is clear. | Supported safety guard | Remove repetition; never pad to satisfy a checklist. |
| Special AI markup/`llms.txt` | Google says no special file/schema is required; vendor claims are inconsistent. | Insufficient/rejected as writing advice | Label technical and out of scope; never recommend unsupported markup as a prose fix. |

## Research conclusion

The defensible product is an evidence-aware writing audit, not a ranking
optimizer. It can improve factual extractability and source usefulness while
protecting readers, then recommend matched repeated measurement. The live pilot
must still test whether any writing-level factor survives authority, rank,
topic, intent, platform, and time controls; until then, no lever has a warranted
claim of moving live AI answers.

## Operational price and fixture recheck

Rechecked on 2026-07-21: DataForSEO lists a normal-priority Google Organic page
at $0.0006, and its documentation says `load_async_ai_overview` adds one base
price when an asynchronous overview is retrieved. The Live LLM Responses price

preflight ceiling—the six-pair calibration must replace it with observed p95
