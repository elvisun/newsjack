Northline expense review pilot

Northline Systems tested an expense-review workflow from January through March 2026.

What the company measured

In the company test of 18,240 expense lines across 12 Canadian offices, reviewers resolved flagged items 14% faster than under the prior workflow.

The test did not measure false-positive rates. According to the company method note, the comparison covered completed reviews only.

  • Population: 18,240 expense lines.
  • Period: January through March 2026.
