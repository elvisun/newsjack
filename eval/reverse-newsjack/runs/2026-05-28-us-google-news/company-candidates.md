## Notes

- Prefer a narrower company over a famous company when both have standing. Reverse eval should test whether the detector can find a story for a plausible client, not whether it can match obvious megacaps.
- Use large companies as alternates when testing whether profile scale changes ranking behavior.
- Avoid picking companies that are the subject of the originating story as the reverse profile unless the test is explicitly about competitor or self-coverage handling.
