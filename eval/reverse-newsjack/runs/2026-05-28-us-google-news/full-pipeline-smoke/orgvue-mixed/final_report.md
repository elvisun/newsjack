## Top Positioning Angles

### 1. Before AI cuts, companies need a workforce model

- **Anchored news:** Wix layoffs (high) and Cloudflare layoffs (moderate)
- **Position:** Orgvue can argue that AI-linked restructuring should start with scenario modeling: which work changes, which roles move, which skills become scarce, and what the operating model looks like after the cut.
- **Journalist shape:** enterprise software, future-of-work, CFO, or HR-tech reporter covering AI-driven restructuring.
- **Use now:** yes, as a developed trend angle. Avoid making Orgvue the judge of whether Wix or Cloudflare made the right call.

### 2. AI workforce strategy is moving from headcount math to operating-model design

- **Anchored news:** AI-driven layoffs as workforce strategy (unknown size) and California AI job-disruption action (moderate)
- **Position:** Orgvue can frame the news around the shift from isolated layoffs to systematic planning: roles, skills, redeployment, governance, and labor-market exposure.
- **Journalist shape:** HR technology, workplace policy, workforce strategy, or labor-market reporter covering employer response to AI disruption.
- **Use now:** yes, especially for HR/workplace trade press and practical policy-response coverage.

## Watch / Not A Fit

- **Develop:** AI making HR faster. Relevant, but needs a sharper contrarian point than "AI should improve quality, not just speed."
- **Monitor:** Visier appointment. Competitor context, not a newsjack unless the follow-on story becomes workforce-transformation or customer-success consolidation.
- **Monitor:** Alibaba MuleRun. Big story, weak Orgvue bridge unless coverage turns toward AI agents changing enterprise workforce design.
- **Reject:** Lakers layoffs. High story size, but off-beat for Orgvue.
