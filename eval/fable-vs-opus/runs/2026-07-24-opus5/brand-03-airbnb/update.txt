Airbnb is launching Airbnb for Teams, a business-travel product. Facts: company travel managers get a central dashboard, consolidated billing, and direct integration with Concur and Ramp for expenses; hosts can opt into a 'business-ready' badge requiring fast wifi, a dedicated workspace, and 24-hour check-in support; Airbnb says business trips are its fastest-growing segment, up 71% year over year off a small base; the feature is free for travelers; rollout starts in the US and UK.
