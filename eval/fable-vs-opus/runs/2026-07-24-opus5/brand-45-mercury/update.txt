Current time: 2026-06-09T16:00:00Z

Mercury is launching personal/consumer bank accounts after years of serving only startups. Facts: Mercury Personal offers checking and savings to individuals, initially invite-only with a waitlist; Mercury became well known as a startup-banking alternative after the collapse of Silicon Valley Bank; banking services are provided through partner banks, not a Mercury bank charter; the company says it is profitable; it serves over 200,000 businesses; consumer-product pricing and deposit targets not disclosed; the move puts it against both neobanks and incumbent banks.
