Instacart says its advertising business hit a $1B annual run rate and is rolling out AI-powered smart carts to more grocery chains. Facts: the Caper smart carts track items as shoppers add them and skip the checkout line; ads now come from over 6,000 consumer brands; Instacart is publicly traded and profitable; the company partners with more than 1,500 retail banners; smart carts are live in a few hundred stores with plans to expand; grocery-delivery order growth has slowed to single digits.
