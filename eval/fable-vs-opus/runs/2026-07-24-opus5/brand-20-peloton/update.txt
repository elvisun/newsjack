Company: Peloton
Current time: 2026-06-09T16:00:00Z

Peloton reported its first profitable quarter in three years and is expanding a low-cost bike-rental program nationwide. Facts: the company posted positive adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow for the quarter; the rental program bundles a bike and membership for one monthly fee with no commitment; subscriber churn fell to a record low; Peloton cut over $200M in annual costs under a new CEO; total members exceed 6 million; the company remains unprofitable on a net basis and carries significant debt.
