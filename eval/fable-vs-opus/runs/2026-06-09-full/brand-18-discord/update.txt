Company: Discord
Current time: 2026-06-09T16:00:00Z

Discord is launching paid creator subscriptions and a revenue-share program for community servers. Facts: server owners can charge monthly fees for premium roles and content; Discord takes a 10% platform fee, below the 30% app-store standard; the company says it has over 200 million monthly active users; the feature targets the creator and gaming-community economy; Discord is privately held and reportedly preparing for an IPO; no creator-earnings data is available yet since the program is new.
