**Glossier more than doubles its Sephora footprint to 1,400 doors**

- **Story type:** data
- **Why a journalist cares:** A beauty-retail trade reporter covering prestige distribution and Sephora's assortment moves. The number is the story: 600 to 1,400 doors is a more-than-doubling of a single retailer relationship, across two countries and two store formats, in one step. This reporter will want the door breakdown and the rollout timeline, not the brand narrative. Not for consumer beauty reviewers, founder-focused press, or general business desks that only care about the channel-strategy question.
- **Why now:** The expansion is being announced now. This is the news of record and it runs on announcement day or not at all.
- **Decay:** 24hr. Standard company-distribution news; once the trade has the number, it is over.
- **What makes it distinct:** News-values lens, magnitude. This is the company-level fact at the bottom rung of the ladder. The other kept angles climb to the category thesis, move to the retailer as protagonist, or shift to the product.
- **Proof it needs:** confirmed door counts split by US Sephora, Canada Sephora, and Sephora at Kohl's; the rollout timeline (all at once or phased through the year); confirmation that Sephora is announcing or at least confirming the same figures.
- **Facts it rests on:** expansion from 600 to 1,400 Sephora doors across North America; the expansion includes US and Canada Sephora locations plus Sephora at Kohl's.

**Glossier spent a decade selling direct. Now it says wholesale is its fastest-growing channel.**

- **Story type:** contrarian
- **Why a journalist cares:** A retail-strategy reporter at a business outlet, or the writer of a DTC and e-commerce newsletter that covers channel economics. The prevailing belief in that world is that a brand born online protects its margin and its customer relationship by staying direct. Here is a brand that did exactly that for ten years and now reports that wholesale, not its own site, is the fastest-growing channel. The reporter's real question is whether wholesale is growing because it is new and small, or because direct has slowed. Not for fragrance or product press, and not for regional retail reporters who only need the door count.
- **Why now:** The expansion announcement is the peg. The channel-shift claim can carry a reported piece for about a week after announcement day if the company puts a number behind it.
- **Decay:** week. Trend and context pieces outlive the announcement, but only while the door news is still fresh.
- **What makes it distinct:** Inversion lens, zoomed up to the category rung. The protagonist is the DTC-to-wholesale shift, with Glossier as the evidence. The door-count angle is the same fact at company altitude; this one asks what the fact says about the model.
- **Proof it needs:** a growth rate or share-of-sales figure for wholesale, even a relative one, since "fastest-growing" with no base is a claim rather than evidence; a Glossier executive on record about why the decade-long DTC-only stance ended; a plain answer to whether direct sales are still growing, because the reporter will ask.
- **Facts it rests on:** Glossier says wholesale is now its fastest-growing channel after a decade as DTC-only; the brand was founded in 2014; the company is privately held and does not disclose revenue or profitability.

**Sephora at Kohl's gets Glossier: what the shop-in-shop deal says about where the brand wants to sell**

- **Story type:** customer-story
- **Why a journalist cares:** A department-store and mass-retail trade reporter who follows the Sephora at Kohl's partnership and which brands it can pull in. The protagonist here is the retailer, not Glossier. Sephora at Kohl's is a different format from a standalone Sephora, and a brand that stayed DTC-only for a decade choosing to enter it is a merchandising decision worth reporting on. Not for DTC strategy newsletters, fragrance press, or Canadian business desks.
- **Why now:** The expansion announcement is the peg. A merchant-side quote from Sephora or Kohl's would let this run a few days after the announcement.
- **Decay:** week. Partnership-format stories can run after announcement day if a retailer voice is available, but the window closes fast once the trade has moved on.
- **What makes it distinct:** Perspective lens, retailer as the customer. The door-count angle treats the 800 new doors as one number; this angle isolates the one format in the list that changes who reaches the brand. It only stands apart if the Kohl's door count is real and material.
- **Proof it needs:** the number of Sephora at Kohl's doors in the 800; whether Kohl's doors carry the full assortment or a subset, including the new fragrances; a Sephora or Kohl's merchant on record, or at least a confirmation of the deal from their side.
- **Facts it rests on:** the wholesale expansion includes Sephora at Kohl's; Glossier was DTC-only for a decade before wholesale.

**Glossier adds three fragrances as it pushes into 800 more Sephora doors**

- **Story type:** trend
- **Why a journalist cares:** A fragrance-category reporter at a beauty trade outlet who tracks line extensions and prestige launches at Sephora. The story is not the three products by themselves. It is that the brand's first fragrance line extension is timed to a more-than-doubling of its retail doors, and the reporter will test whether fragrance is the category Glossier is using to fill those shelves. Not for retail-strategy press, department-store reporters, or general business desks.
- **Why now:** The three products launch alongside the expansion, so the launch date is the hook.
- **Decay:** 24hr. A product launch is over in a day for the trade unless sell-through data follows later.
- **What makes it distinct:** Perspective lens, product as protagonist. Every other kept angle is about distribution. This one is about what is going into the doors, and it is the only angle a fragrance-beat reporter can use.
- **Proof it needs:** the three product names, formats, and prices; whether the extension is exclusive to Sephora or also sold on Glossier's own site; whether the fragrances go into all 1,400 doors or a subset; what the original fragrance this extends did in wholesale, if the company will say.
- **Facts it rests on:** Glossier is launching its first fragrance line extension; three new fragrance products launch alongside the expansion; the expansion takes Glossier from 600 to 1,400 Sephora doors.

**Refused angles**

- "From beauty blog to 1,400 Sephora doors: the Glossier founder story" — `duplicate`. The fact block names no founder and includes no founder quote, so the blog-to-wholesale arc is the same story as the contrarian angle until a named founder goes on record about ending the DTC-only decade.
- "Glossier expands across Sephora Canada" — `duplicate`. Same door-count story with a geographic label; there is no Canadian door split to make it a separate story.
- "What wholesale growth means for a Glossier exit or IPO" — `hallucinated_fact`. The company is privately held and discloses no revenue or profitability; nothing in the facts supports any exit story.
- "DTC is dead and Glossier proves it" — `hallucinated_fact`. One company's self-reported, unnumbered channel claim is not evidence about a market. The contrarian angle above stays at the level the facts support.
- "Glossier takes on rival brands at Sephora" — `hallucinated_fact`. No competitor appears in the facts.
- "Fragrance is beauty's hottest category and Glossier is chasing it" — `hallucinated_fact`. No category data was supplied.
- "Glossier transforms beauty retail with landmark Sephora expansion" — `slop`.
- Any newsjack tying the expansion to a current retail or beauty headline — `no_why_now_but_required`. No live news signal was supplied, and reaching for one without it is forced relevance.

**Uncomfortable questions**

- "Fastest-growing channel" growing from what base? If you will not give a growth rate or a wholesale share of sales, the strongest angle here is a claim you cannot defend, and reporters will ask whether direct sales have slowed.
- How many of the 800 new doors are Sephora at Kohl's, and how many are in Canada? Without the split, two of the four angles are thinner than they look.
- Are the three fragrances going into all 1,400 doors, and are they Sephora-exclusive? The fragrance angle depends on that answer.
- Who goes on record: a Glossier executive, a Sephora merchant, anyone at Kohl's? Retailer silence weakens the Kohl's angle badly.
- When exactly did wholesale start? "A decade as DTC-only" from a 2014 founding will be checked against the date of the first Sephora doors.
- Has any of this already been reported or announced? No prior coverage was provided and none could be checked here, so the freshness of every angle is unverified.
- Is there an embargo on the expansion or the fragrance launch? If so, prep only; nothing sends before lift.

**Next step:** `journalist-fit-check`. Four viable shapes exist. Pick one, then resolve it against real named reporters on that beat. If you pick the contrarian angle, get the wholesale number first, or it will not survive the fit check.
