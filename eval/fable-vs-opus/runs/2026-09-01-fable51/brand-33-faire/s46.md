## Uncomfortable questions

1. **What is the actual default rate on Faire retailer orders?** Without this, the risk angle will be asked by a reporter before you volunteer it. A financing product with no disclosed default rate is either a transparency gap or a defensible proprietary number — either way, you need a position before pitching.
2. **Do brands opt in to upfront payment, or is it automatic — and do they pay for it?** If Faire charges brands a fee or adjusts their take-home rate to fund the float, the brand angle changes substantially. If it's free to brands, the cost structure question moves entirely to Angle C.
3. **What is the enforcement mechanism when a retailer doesn't pay at 60 days?** Marketplace suspension, credit reporting, collections? This is part of the product and a gap in current facts that a reporter will ask immediately.
4. **What is the source methodology behind "cash flow is the top barrier to ordering"?** A published survey, internal purchase-data analysis, or anecdotal finding each carry different credibility. Without a citable source, Angle B depends on Faire's word.
5. **How does Faire fund the float?** Its own balance sheet, a warehouse credit facility, or securitization each imply different risk profiles and scalability limits. A fintech reporter will ask this in the first email.
