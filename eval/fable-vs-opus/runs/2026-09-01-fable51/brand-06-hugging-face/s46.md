## Uncomfortable questions

- The benchmark claim is the most attention-getting fact in this update, but it rests entirely on Hugging Face's own numbers. What is the plan if independent researchers reproduce it at lower performance? Pitching the "matches a 4x larger model" claim before independent confirmation means pitching a headline that could be corrected publicly within days.
- The Fortune 100 manufacturer is described as "named" but the name was not supplied here. Is the company willing to be named in press coverage, or is this a reference-customer arrangement that permits anonymous citation only? The customer-story and category-creation angles both depend on moving the manufacturer from unnamed to on-record.
- What exactly are the "common reasoning evals"? Reporters covering AI benchmarks will ask for the specific suite and methodology within the first reply. An answer of "standard benchmarks" will not hold.
- The enterprise tier adds private model hosting — does "private" mean hosted on Hugging Face infrastructure with logical isolation, or customer-managed deployment on the customer's own infrastructure? The answer materially changes the data-sovereignty and vendor-dependency story.
- Has the open-source contributor community reacted to the paid enterprise tier? The tension between open-source culture and enterprise monetization is a known flashpoint in developer communities; a reporter will look for that reaction whether or not you surface it.
