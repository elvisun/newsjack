## Uncomfortable Questions

1. **Is Deel the plaintiff or the defendant in the corporate espionage lawsuit?** The facts say "in a lawsuit with a competitor" — that's ambiguous, and the answer changes the counterposition angle from aggressive competitor to embattled defender.
2. **Why is accuracy and audit data not disclosed?** Is it because the data hasn't been collected, because the results are internal, or because it doesn't exist yet? This is the central vulnerability for the contrarian angle and for the company's credibility with any journalist who runs the accountability story.
3. **Are any existing customers willing to be named?** Without one, the trend angle is thinner, the customer-story is off the table entirely, and every angle rests on Deel's own claims about what the tool does.
4. **What happens after the AI flags a risk?** Does it recommend an action, route to a human compliance reviewer, or stop at the flag? The mechanism matters — an AI that flags and stops is different from one that makes a recommendation.
5. **What do new customers pay for this feature?** "Included for existing customers" doesn't answer whether it's a permanent plan feature or a launch-period perk. The business model angle needs this.
