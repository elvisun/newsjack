## Uncomfortable questions

- Are any of the restaurant owners who complained about fees willing to be named and quoted? Without a named source, the captive-market and default-rate angles depend entirely on the disclosure gap, which is real but thinner without a human face.
- What are Toast Capital's actual fees? The advance structure (repaid as share of daily sales) is disclosed, but the effective cost of capital is not in the update. That number is the difference between a story about an innovative product and a story about an expensive one.
- When exactly did Toast reach profitability, and is gross margin by segment disclosed in public filings? The investor angle depends on whether the "fintech is higher-margin" claim is in the financials or a management assertion not yet tested against disclosed numbers.
- How large is the Toast Capital loan book? Ten million dollars outstanding is a feature; one billion dollars outstanding is a systemic small-business finance story. The update gives no size.
- How does the payroll product handle state-by-state tipped minimum wage variation specifically? If the product does not solve that, the HR angle loses its differentiation claim and may not be distinct enough from general small-business payroll software.
