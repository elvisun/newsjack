Not for consumer-tech how-to desks, generic AI-hype newsletters, or design-inspiration blogs.

- **Decay:** 24hr — a launch is a one-day news event; the broader incumbent-vs-challenger framing can stretch a few days if a reporter gets a real customer or an incumbent response.
- **What makes it distinct:** Perspective lens, incumbent/competitor protagonist — the story is the category power balance (Canva vs Adobe and Microsoft), not Canva's product features or its scale numbers. Different protagonist from the data and the AI-acquisition angles below.
