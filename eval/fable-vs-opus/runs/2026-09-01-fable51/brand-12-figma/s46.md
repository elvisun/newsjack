- **"AI will make designers and developers obsolete"** — `hallucinated_fact`. This would require inventing a thesis and evidence not present in the facts. The update is a product launch announcement, not a displacement study.

## Uncomfortable questions

1. **Is the code production-quality or prototype-quality?** This is the most important unknown. If Make exports clean, accessible, performant React, angle B (developer workflow) is a real business story. If it exports messy scaffolding, it's a nice demo. The answer determines what kind of reporters will care.
2. **Can any named Fortune 500 customer go on record about testing Make?** Without one, the developer-workflow angle and the company-trajectory angle both thin out significantly.
3. **What does the full pricing stack look like?** The $25/seat is described as "on top of existing plans" — but which plans, at what existing price, and for which user roles? Reporters and readers will immediately ask, and a monetization angle without the full picture is incomplete.
4. **Has Figma leadership explicitly connected this launch to their post-Adobe trajectory on the record?** A CEO or CPO quote linking Make to the company's independent path is what turns angle A from inference to story. Without it, a reporter has to do that work themselves, and many won't.
5. **What is the IPO posture?** "No IPO timing disclosed" is a meaningful negative signal to some investors and SaaS reporters. Has Figma said anything about its independent future — fundraising, profitability, or a longer-term horizon? Without any context, angle D's investor hook is thinner than it needs to be.
