Company: Patagonia
Current time: 2026-06-09T16:00:00Z

Patagonia is making its Worn Wear repair program available in all US stores and launching mail-in repairs nationwide. Facts: customers can drop off any Patagonia item for repair at 70+ US stores or mail it in; basic repairs are free, complex ones charged at cost; the company says its resale platform Worn Wear has now resold over 500,000 used items; Patagonia is privately held and directs profits to environmental causes; no new apparel line announced; the company will not say what share of revenue resale represents.
