Not for consumer tech press, general startup blogs, or aviation-regulation specialists — this is an operations story, not a flight-safety or policy story.
- **Why now:** The launch itself is the hook — this is the news event.
- **Decay:** week — the launch triggers coverage now, but the underlying "utilities are automating grid inspection" trend can support context pieces for several days after.
- **What makes it distinct:** News-values (relevance) lens plus a ladder zoom into the operational rung — this is about the inspection use case itself, not the company's business strategy or the policy fight around it.
- **Proof it needs:** a named utility customer or a detailed anonymized case; a real comparison to manual/piloted inspection (time, cost, or defect-catch rate); clarity on what "several US utilities" actually means — how many, and are they live deployments or pilots.
- **Facts it rests on:** the drone docks, charges, and runs scheduled inspection flights of power lines and substations; it sends defect reports automatically; it flies with no pilot; Skydio says it has contracts with several US utilities.
