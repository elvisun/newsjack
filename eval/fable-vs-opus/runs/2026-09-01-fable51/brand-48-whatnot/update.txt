Company: Whatnot
Current time: 2026-06-09T16:00:00Z

Whatnot says it crossed $3B in annual livestream-commerce sales and is expanding beyond collectibles into fashion and electronics. Facts: sellers run live video auctions and shoppers buy in real time; Whatnot takes a commission on sales; the platform started with trading cards and collectibles and is now adding new categories; the company is privately held and last valued at nearly $5B; it says live commerce is far larger in Asia and it is betting the US catches up; seller-earnings distribution and take-rate not fully disclosed.
