Company: On
Current time: 2026-06-09T16:00:00Z

On is launching a nationwide subscription for its fully recyclable Cyclon running shoe. Facts: subscribers pay a monthly fee, run in the shoe, then mail it back to be ground down and remade rather than owning it; the Cyclon is made from a castor-bean-based material; On is publicly traded, profitable, and one of the fastest-growing athletic brands; the subscription was previously a limited pilot; the company says it wants to close the loop on shoe waste; recycling-rate and per-unit economics not disclosed.
