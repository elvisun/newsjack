Company: Chipotle
Current time: 2026-06-09T16:00:00Z

Chipotle is rolling out Autocado, an avocado-processing robot, to 100 restaurants and holding menu prices flat for the year. Facts: Autocado roughly halves guacamole prep time in pilot kitchens; Chipotle says it will not raise menu prices in the coming year despite food-cost inflation; the chain has over 3,500 locations; it is also testing a robotic bowl-assembly system called the Digital Makeline; no jobs will be cut, the company says, with staff redeployed to the front line; labor-cost figures not disclosed.
