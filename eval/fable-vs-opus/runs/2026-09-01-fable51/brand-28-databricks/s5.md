- **Story type:** category-creation
- **Why a journalist cares:** An enterprise-AI or infrastructure reporter at a business-tech or enterprise trade outlet who covers AI adoption inside regulated industries — finance, healthcare, insurance — and tracks vendor and compliance risk in AI purchasing decisions. This reporter can test whether "open and inspectable" is a real purchasing driver for compliance teams or just a talking point. Not for consumer AI press, general startup news, or outlets that only cover model leaderboards.
- **Why now:** The open-license release is the news hook, today.
- **Decay:** week — a trend/context piece, not breaking news.
- **What makes it distinct:** Ladder lens, zoomed up, combined with the relevance news value. The protagonist is the regulated-enterprise buyer and the adoption trend, not Databricks' finances (below) or its rivals (below).
- **Proof it needs:** a named regulated-industry customer willing to describe why "open and inspectable" mattered in a real purchasing decision; the actual license terms — whether enterprises can truly modify and redistribute, or "open" is narrower than it sounds; independent, non-Databricks benchmark results, since the only numbers supplied are the company's own.
- **Facts it rests on:** the model is released under an open license for enterprises to fine-tune on their own data; it's positioned as an alternative to closed APIs for regulated enterprises.
