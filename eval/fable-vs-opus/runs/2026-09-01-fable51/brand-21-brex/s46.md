## Uncomfortable questions

1. Can Brex provide at least one named enterprise customer willing to go on record about international expense management pain? All four angles sharpen with a real voice; the customer-story angle effectively cannot be pitched without one.
2. What does "local reimbursement" mean mechanically — are employees paid from a local Brex-held account in local currency, or is it FX conversion routed through US-based infrastructure? The answer changes what the competitive story actually is.
3. How does Brex's 50-country coverage compare specifically to Ramp's current international footprint, and to Amex or Citi global corporate programs? The counterposition and trend angles need a real comparison, not an assumed one.
4. Will Brex share any revenue or ARR data? Without it, the valuation-mechanics angle rests on the valuation number alone — thin ground for a serious analysis piece.
5. How did Brex build the local-currency infrastructure — banking licenses in each country, card network passporting, BaaS partners? A fintech reporter will ask this first; the answer is what separates a real global product from a press release claim.
