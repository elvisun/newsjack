Company: Allbirds
Current time: 2026-06-09T16:00:00Z

Allbirds is relaunching with a new natural-materials shoe platform and reported its first quarter of gross-margin improvement under a turnaround plan. Facts: the new platform uses a sugarcane-and-wool blend the company says is more durable than its original wool runner; Allbirds closed underperforming stores and cut its product range by 40%; the company is publicly traded, unprofitable, and trades well below its IPO price; gross margin improved but it remains net-loss-making; a named retail wholesale partnership is part of the relaunch.
