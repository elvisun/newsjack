**Uncomfortable questions**

- Which stablecoin and which partner? Three of the five angles have a hole where the instrument should be, and a policy or category reporter will not run a story on an unnamed asset.
- Which market goes live first, under which approval, and which markets are still waiting? Without that, the regulator angle is a theory about a rollout that has not started.
- "Faster and cheaper than correspondent banking": measured against what corridor, what amount, and by how much? And is the comparison against banks or against Wise's own existing rail?
