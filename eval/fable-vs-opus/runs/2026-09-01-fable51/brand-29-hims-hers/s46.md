**Uncomfortable questions**

- What are the actual prices? "A fraction of branded-drug prices" is a marketing phrase. No reporter will run the cost-access angle without a monthly dollar figure for Hims versus Ozempic or Wegovy. The number is required before this angle is pitchable.
- Who makes the compounded semaglutide? The unnamed supply source is the single largest gap in this update. Every regulatory reporter and every investor will ask on the first call. A supplier under active FDA scrutiny is a different story than a reputable outsourcing facility. Not naming it is a red flag, not neutral.
- What does "a licensed provider reviews every prescription" mean operationally? Is this a synchronous telehealth visit with clinical evaluation, or an asynchronous form review? The FDA's concern about compounded GLP-1s centers on the adequacy of oversight. The actual process determines whether the regulatory angle is a defensible story or a liability story.
- Is there clinical evidence that Hims's compounded semaglutide produces comparable outcomes to branded versions, or is the company's position that it is clinically appropriate without equivalence data? The honest answer changes every angle here.
- What does the at-home blood testing measure specifically, and what happens to the treatment plan as a result? Without specifics, this is a product feature in a press release, not a differentiator.
