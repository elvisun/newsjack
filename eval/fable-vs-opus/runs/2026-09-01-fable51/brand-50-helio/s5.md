**Helio raised $7M to "detect wildfires within minutes" — with zero satellites in orbit and no named customer**

- **Story type:** contrarian
- **Why a journalist cares:** A space-industry or climate-tech accountability reporter who writes the skeptical sidebar or standalone reality-check piece alongside funding news — someone whose outlet has a track record of testing claims against flight heritage rather than rewriting the press release. Not for the company's own trade partners, general funding-roundup blogs, or outlets that only run announcement rewrites.
- **Why now:** The gap between the promise and the disclosed status is sharpest right as other coverage runs the celebratory version of this same raise.
- **Decay:** week — the gap between claim and reality doesn't close overnight, so there's room to report it out rather than rush a same-day take.
- **What makes it distinct:** Inversion/contrarian lens — it runs directly against the implicit promise in the funding news, using the company's own disclosed gaps as the evidence rather than restating the pitch.
- **Proof it needs:** clarification on whether "detect wildfires within minutes" is a demonstrated capability or a design goal — any ground test, simulation, or prior-company result it's based on; the identity and terms of the state fire agency MOU; a launch timeline, if one exists.
- **Facts it rests on:** no satellite is in orbit yet and there are no operational results; revenue, a named agency customer, and a launch date are not disclosed; the company's own claim that its satellites will detect wildfires within minutes and alert agencies faster than current systems; an MOU with one (unnamed) state fire agency.

**Refused angles**

- "Helio's satellites will revolutionize wildfire response" — `slop`.
- A customer-story angle built around the state fire agency's experience with the technology — `hallucinated_fact`. The agency isn't named and there are no operational results yet; writing this angle would mean inventing outcomes that don't exist.
- Taking "alerts agencies faster than current systems" as an established scale/magnitude claim — `hallucinated_fact`. No satellite has flown; the speed claim is unverified company positioning, not a fact to headline.
- A government-procurement trend angle built solely on the MOU — `no_journalist_shape`. Without knowing which state or agency signed it, there's no real sub-beat or outlet to name; a single unnamed MOU doesn't support a full angle on its own.

**Uncomfortable questions**

- Will Helio name the state fire agency, and will that agency go on record about why it signed an MOU with a pre-launch satellite company?
- Is "detect wildfires within minutes" a demonstrated capability or a design target — has it been validated through simulation, ground testing, or prior work at Planet Labs or SpaceX?
- What's the actual launch date, and is there a signed launch contract, or is "small satellites" still at the design stage?
- With no disclosed revenue, is the MOU the company's only commercial signal, or are there others in progress?
- What did the founders specifically do at SpaceX and Planet Labs? That determines whether the founder-pedigree angle is about direct relevant experience or a more generic space-industry résumé.
