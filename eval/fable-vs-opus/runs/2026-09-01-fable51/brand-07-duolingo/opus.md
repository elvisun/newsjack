Not for hard-news AI-policy desks or general startup blogs.

- **Decay:** 24hr — the metric is tied to the launch announcement; it reads as same-day company news unless a reporter builds a longer engagement-trend piece around it.

- **Why now:** The new video feature and chess course are launching now, giving a reporter a concrete peg to revisit the gamification-vs-learning debate.
- **Decay:** week — the launch is the peg, but the learning-quality debate is a context piece that holds for several days.
- **What makes it distinct:** Inversion/contrarian lens — it names the educators' critique (gamification as shallow learning) and tests the new features against it, rather than reporting the features as wins. Distinct protagonist (critics / learners) from the data and feature angles.
- **Proof it needs:** which educators or what bodies have made the criticism, and where (the fact says only that "some educators" have, with no source) — a reporter needs at least one nameable critic and one piece of independent learning-outcome evidence; Duolingo's own on-record response to the shallow-learning charge.
- **Facts it rests on:** some educators have publicly criticized gamification as shallow learning; the company is adding AI spoken video conversation practice and launching a chess course.
