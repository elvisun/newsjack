**Uncomfortable questions**

- What is the absolute DAU figure? The 40% growth claim is stronger with the base number behind it; without it, the company controls all the framing.
- Who specifically are the educators on record criticizing Duolingo's gamification? "Some educators" is not a pitch anchor — named critics make the contrarian angle usable.
- Why chess? Is Duolingo planning to add more non-language subjects? The platform-pivot angle collapses to a single ambiguous data point without an answer.
- Does Duolingo have any learning outcomes data — fluency test scores, third-party assessments, user-reported proficiency gains — or only engagement metrics? Nine minutes of practice is time spent, not language acquired.
- What is the Max subscription price, and how many paid subscribers does Duolingo have? The business-model and data angles both need these to move beyond the company's own framing.
- Is the 40% growth figure from an official SEC filing or earnings call, or a marketing press release? That distinction matters to a markets reporter using it as a primary claim.
