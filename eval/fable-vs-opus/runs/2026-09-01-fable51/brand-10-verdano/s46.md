## Uncomfortable questions

- Can any of the three utility pilot customers be named? Without one, the customer story is unavailable and every other angle is materially weaker. "Three paying utility customers in pilot" is an assertion; a named reference is evidence.
- What is the name of the lead seed fund? An unnamed fund cannot anchor a funding-mechanics story. A reporter needs a partner name and a fund name. This gap disables the strongest VC-angle pitch.
- Has Verdano disclosed any pilot results — load shifted, grid events avoided, curtailment reduced, cost savings — even on background or under embargo? "Predicts local solar generation and shifts flexible loads to cut grid strain" describes the approach. Without an outcome metric, the AI's effectiveness is an unverified claim, which weakens every angle.
- Which co-founder came from Tesla Energy and which from NREL? The founder-profile angle needs attributable roles and dates. "Ex-Tesla Energy and ex-NREL founders" is currently anonymous.
- Is the community-solar coordination gap documented anywhere independently? The category-creation angle rests on the claim that this tier lacks adequate software. That claim needs a sourced basis the user can hand to a reporter — not Verdano's own marketing materials.
