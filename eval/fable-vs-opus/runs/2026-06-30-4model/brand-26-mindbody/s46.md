## Uncomfortable questions

- Can the 22% no-show reduction be independently verified? What was the pilot sample size, duration, and methodology — and was it conducted by Mindbody or a third party? A self-reported metric from a private company with no external validation will be the first thing a skeptical reporter challenges.
- What is the actual relationship between this AI tool and ClassPass? Does it draw on ClassPass member data to fill studio slots, or are the two systems entirely separate? If ClassPass members are already a mechanism for filling empty spots, is the AI solving a problem ClassPass already addresses — or a different one?
- Who owns Mindbody after the take-private, and when did the deal happen? Without that, the platform-strategy angle has no financial frame and loses the half of the story a business desk would need to publish it.
- Can any pilot studio be named? A specific boutique studio with a real owner and a real result transforms the data angle from a press-claim story into a reported one.
- Does the 22% figure reflect the text-fill mechanism alone, the scheduling optimization alone, or the combination? If they cannot be separated, the metric becomes harder to defend when a reporter asks the obvious follow-up.
