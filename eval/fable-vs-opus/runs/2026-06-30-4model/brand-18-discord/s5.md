- **Why now:** Tied to this feature's launch.
- **Decay:** 24hr — this rides the launch news cycle; it's not evergreen and there's no supplied signal to justify calling it faster-moving than a standard company-news window.
- **What makes it distinct:** Perspective/conflict lens — the fee structure itself is the protagonist, positioned against the app-store status quo. Distinct from the IPO and category angles below, which use different protagonists.
