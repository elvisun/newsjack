## Uncomfortable Questions

- What was Whatnot's GMV a year or two years ago? Without a growth rate, "$3B" is a number without a trajectory, and every reporter writing the live-commerce progress piece will ask for it. The milestone angle breaks without this.
- Is $3B GMV or net revenue to Whatnot? These are not the same figure, and the press frequently conflates them. A reporter will check. Clarify before pitching any story that leads with the number.
- Who inside Whatnot will go on record about the Asia convergence bet — specifically with a named exec, a specific market size comparison, and a timeline? Without attribution, the trend angle rests on a company claim with no face behind it.
- What does the average Whatnot seller earn? The seller-economics angle is real only if named sellers or earnings distribution data can be sourced. Without it the human story is thin and the reporter has to do all the work of finding subjects.
- Which specific fashion and electronics subcategories is Whatnot entering, and what does the live-auction mechanic change in those categories versus a static listing on Poshmark or eBay? The competitive-disruption angle needs this to name which incumbents are actually threatened rather than vaguely adjacent.
