## Uncomfortable questions

- Is there a named paid member who can describe the full workflow on record — the alert, the AI-generated itinerary, the decision to book, and where they ended up? Without a real human example, the "turns alert into itinerary" story is a product spec, not a story. Angles 1 and 3 both weaken significantly without this.

- "No airline or hotel booking happens in-app yet" — is in-app booking on the roadmap, and is Going willing to say so on record? The word "yet" implies it. If a founder confirms it publicly, angle 3 sharpens. If Going declines to confirm, a reporter will notice the hedge and it becomes part of the story.
- How large is the "smaller paid Premium/Elite base"? The freemium conversion angle reads very differently at 200,000 paid members versus 20,000. The current phrasing does not give a reporter enough to work with.
