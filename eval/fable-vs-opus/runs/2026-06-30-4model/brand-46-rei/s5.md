**REI takes gear trade-ins and repairs from a pilot program to all 180+ stores**

- **Story type:** customer-story
- **Why a journalist cares:** A retail-ops or outdoor-industry trade reporter covering how physical stores handle circular-economy programs at scale — moving trade-in/repair from "online and select stores" to every location is an operations story (staffing, in-store logistics, credit mechanics), not just a marketing line. Not for labor reporters, investor/markets press, or general lifestyle desks looking for a feel-good weekend piece.
- **Why now:** The expansion to all 180+ stores is the news itself, announced alongside the dividend as of today.
- **Decay:** 24hr — standard company-news rollout; goes stale once the announcement cycle passes.
- **What makes it distinct:** Perspective lens, customer/member protagonist, zoomed to the concrete in-store experience — the other angles below zoom out to money, labor, or category.
- **Proof it needs:** confirmed rollout timeline (when the last stores went live); whether the credit/repair benefit is member-exclusive or open to any shopper; what "repairs" covers (bikes only? tents, boots, all gear?); a member or store-employee account of how it actually works at the counter.
- **Facts it rests on:** members can trade used gear for store credit and get repairs at any of REI's 180+ stores; the program previously ran online and in select stores.
