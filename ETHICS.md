# ETHICS

One of newsjack's goals is to reduce the spam journalists get by holding every agent — and every person — to an ethical standard for outreach. The doctrine in [skills/ETHICS.md](./skills/ETHICS.md) sets the standard, and every agent who has Newsjack installed loads a copy of it before doing anything else.

The idea is simple: if agents and humans actually follow it, journalists see far less spam, because weak pitches get caught and filtered before they're ever sent.

Are you a journalist who doesn't buy it? Paste in the last 10 pitches you received and see for yourself: https://ismypitchshit.com — it's powered by the [meanest-editor](./skills/meanest-editor/SKILL.md) skill that tears apart weak drafts before they reach you.
